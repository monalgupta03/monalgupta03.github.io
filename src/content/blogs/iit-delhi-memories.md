# Farewell IIT Delhi: Memories and Lessons

**Date:** July 15, 2024

Looking back at four incredible years at IIT Delhi...

## The Journey Begins

When I first stepped onto the IIT Delhi campus, I had no idea how much this place would shape me. Four years later, I'm leaving with memories, friendships, and lessons that will last a lifetime.

## Academic Highlights

- Discovered my passion for systems programming
- Built projects that taught me more than any textbook
- Found mentors who believed in my potential

## Beyond the Classroom

The best learning happened outside lectures:
- Late-night hackathons
- Student club activities
- Chai breaks with friends discussing everything from philosophy to code

## Lessons Learned

1. **Fail fast, learn faster** - Not every project succeeded, but every failure taught something
2. **Community matters** - The people around you shape your journey
3. **Stay curious** - The best engineers never stop learning

## What's Next

As I close this chapter, I'm excited for what lies ahead. Bristol awaits, and new adventures are calling.

---

*To everyone at IIT Delhi - thank you for everything.*
